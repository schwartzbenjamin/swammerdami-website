I’LL SEE YOU AGAIN IN 25 YEARS.

I've a newfound interest in backing up and losslessly transferring old videotapes. This page lists my current and ongoing media archiving efforts.

Projects

  • 2026 - 2099

    The Cooper Collection

    A Sandpoint-based filmmaker has a massive collection of tapes -- 7000 hours shot for local television programing. The footage is an incredible piece of history spanning the 80s to the 10s. It currently exists across several formats: VHS, S-VHS, VHS-C miniDV, HDV, DVCAM, and DVD. Currently, we are working to get proper funding and get the ball really rolling on this massive digitization project.

    The Sandpoint Centennial Film

    Successfully transferred about 200 hours of miniDV footage and interviews. These materials were shot by Erik Daarstad for a documentary celebrating the centennial of Sandpoint, Idaho.
